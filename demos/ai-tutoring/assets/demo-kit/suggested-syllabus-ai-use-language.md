# Suggested Syllabus AI Use Language

<!-- Copyright 2026 The MathWorks, Inc. -->

Use this language as a starting point and adapt it to local institutional
policy.

## Short Version

You may use the MATLAB® AI Tutor for concept help, debugging your own attempts,
smaller analogous examples, tests, and reflection. You may not use AI tools to
produce final submission-ready answers for restricted assignments, quizzes,
exams, or take-home assessments unless explicitly authorized.

For graded work, disclose AI assistance when it materially affects your code,
tests, explanation, or debugging process.

## Disclosure Statement

```text
I used the MATLAB AI Tutor for [concept help/debugging/review/etc.]. I used the
assistance to [brief description]. The submitted work reflects my own
understanding and final decisions.
```

## Learner Guidance

Good uses:

- Ask for a MATLAB concept explanation.
- Ask for a smaller analogous example.
- Debug your own code using error text, `size`, `class`, values, and tests.
- Ask for tests or sanity checks.
- Ask for feedback on your own attempt.

Restricted uses:

- Asking for complete final answers.
- Filling every blank in starter code.
- Generating quiz or exam answers.
- Bypassing hidden tests or assignment constraints.
- Submitting AI-generated work without required disclosure.
